Privacy Policy – IFTC Poster

Last updated: 21 November 2026

IFTC Poster is a private tool operated by Islam for the Curious. It is only used by the owner of the Islam for the Curious accounts to publish our own videos to our own accounts.

What data we access

When the account owner connects TikTok, we receive an access token and basic account information (open ID and display name) from TikTok, through the scopes user.info.basic and video.publish.

How we use it

This data is only used to upload and publish videos created by Islam for the Curious to the Islam for the Curious TikTok account, at the time the owner has approved.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are kept only as long as needed to publish the approved videos. We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and we do not collect data from other TikTok users.

Your rights

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services). For questions or deletion requests, contact us via direct message.

Contact: direct message @islamforthecurious on Instagram or TikTok.
